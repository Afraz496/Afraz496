### About me

- 🔭 Building **ML-driven epidemiological surveillance pipelines** — anomaly/signal detection, automated data-quality gates, and LLM narration of results
- 🩺 Working on **clinical NLP** — fine-tuning BioBERT / ClinicalBERT on free-text clinician notes
- 🕸️ Developing **Temporal Graph Neural Networks** for pandemic forecasting, alongside spatial ML for respiratory illness forecasting
- 🤖 Exploring **LLM agents** for detecting and disentangling public health misinformation
- 📈 Running a **time series reading group** on forecasting and uncertainty quantification
- 🚀 Co-founder of **[Gresp](https://gresp.ca)**
- 👯 Open to collaborating on **applied ML, forecasting, and health-data projects**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I built my own PC for fun
